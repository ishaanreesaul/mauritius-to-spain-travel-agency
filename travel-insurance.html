Important Information

Schengen Visa Requirements

Travel insurance is mandatory for Schengen visa applications from Mauritius. Minimum coverage of €30,000 required for medical emergencies and repatriation.

Pre-existing Conditions

Declare all pre-existing medical conditions when purchasing insurance. Some conditions may require additional premium or special coverage.

Claim Process

Keep all receipts and documentation. Contact our assistance line immediately in case of emergency. We guide you through the entire claims process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is travel insurance mandatory for Spain?

Yes, for Mauritian citizens, travel insurance with minimum €30,000 medical coverage is mandatory for Schengen visa application to Spain.

Can I purchase insurance after booking my trip?

Yes, but we recommend purchasing insurance at the time of booking to ensure coverage for any pre-trip cancellations.

Are adventure activities covered?

Standard plans cover basic activities. For adventure sports like skiing or hiking, additional coverage may be required.
