Booking Terms & Conditions

Important information about your booking with SpainVoyage

1. Booking Process

1.1 How to Book

Bookings can be made through:

  • Our website contact form
  • Email: bookings@spainvoyage.mu
  • Phone: +230 123 4567
  • In person at our Port Louis office

1.2 Booking Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed when:

  1. We receive your completed booking form
  2. We receive the required deposit (see Section 2)
  3. We issue a written confirmation via email

Note: Verbal confirmations are not binding. Only written confirmations constitute a confirmed booking.

1.3 Booking Requirements

To make a booking, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Have a valid passport with at least 6 months validity beyond your return date
  • Provide accurate personal information for all travelers
  • Agree to these terms and conditions

Full and final payment is due 45 days before departure. If full payment is not received by this date, we reserve the right to:

  • Cancel your booking

2.4 Price Guarantee

Once your booking is confirmed and deposit paid, your package price is guaranteed against any currency fluctuations or price increases, except for:

  • Government-imposed taxes or fees
  • Fuel surcharges imposed by airlines
  • Changes requested by you

Any such increases will be notified to you before being applied.

Important: Some airlines, hotels, and tour operators may impose stricter cancellation policies. In such cases, their policies will apply and may result in higher cancellation fees.

3.2 Cancellation by Us

We reserve the right to cancel your booking in exceptional circumstances, including:

  • Force majeure events (war, natural disasters, pandemics)
  • If you fail to make payments on time
  • If the minimum number of participants for a group tour is not met

If we cancel your booking (except for non-payment), we will offer:

  • A full refund of all monies paid, or
  • An alternative travel date or package of equivalent value

3.3 Refund Processing

Refunds will be processed within 14-21 business days of receiving your cancellation request. Refunds will be made to the original payment method where possible.

4. Changes & Amendments

4.1 Changes by You

If you wish to make changes to your confirmed booking:

  • More than 60 days before departure: Free of charge (subject to availability)
  • 30-60 days before departure: MUR 1,500 administration fee per change
  • Less than 30 days before departure: Treated as cancellation and rebooking

4.2 Name Changes

Name changes are subject to airline and hotel policies and may incur additional fees. Most airlines charge for name changes, and some may not permit them at all.

4.3 Changes by Us

While we strive to provide all services as confirmed, we may occasionally need to make changes. We will notify you of any significant changes as soon as possible. Significant changes include:

  • Change of departure or return time by more than 12 hours
  • Change of departure airport (except between airports serving the same city)
  • Change of accommodation to a lower category
  • Significant change to the itinerary

If we make a significant change, you may:

  • Accept the changed arrangements
  • Accept an alternative package (paying or receiving a refund for any price difference)
  • Cancel and receive a full refund

5. Responsibilities

5.1 Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Ensuring all travelers have valid passports and necessary visas
  • Checking visa requirements and applying in time
  • Providing accurate personal information
  • Purchasing adequate travel insurance
  • Checking travel advice and requirements (vaccinations, etc.)
  • Arriving at airports on time for all flights
  • Complying with all laws and regulations in Spain

5.2 Our Responsibilities

We are responsible for:

  • Providing the services described in your booking confirmation
  • Assisting with visa applications (if included in your package)
  • Providing emergency contact numbers
  • Offering reasonable assistance if problems occur during your trip

Note: We act as an agent for airlines, hotels, and other service providers. Their terms and conditions also apply to your booking.

5.3 Liability Limitations

Our liability for any loss or damage you suffer is limited to:

  • Twice the price of your package (excluding insurance premiums and amendment charges)
  • For non-package arrangements, the cost of the specific arrangement in question

We are not liable for:

  • Loss or damage caused by events beyond our reasonable control
  • Loss or damage caused by your own actions or omissions
  • Indirect or consequential losses

6. Travel Insurance

6.1 Insurance Requirement

Travel insurance is mandatory for all SpainVoyage bookings. You must purchase adequate travel insurance covering:

  • Medical expenses (minimum €30,000 for Schengen visa)
  • Repatriation costs
  • Cancellation and curtailment
  • Personal liability
  • Loss of baggage and personal effects
  • Travel delay

You must provide proof of insurance before travel documents are issued.

6.2 Our Insurance Offer

We offer comprehensive travel insurance through our partner, Allianz Travel. Our insurance packages are specifically designed for Mauritian travelers to Spain and include:

  • Schengen visa-compliant coverage
  • COVID-19 coverage (medical and cancellation)
  • 24/7 emergency assistance in Spanish, French, and Creole
  • Direct billing with Spanish hospitals

You may purchase insurance from us or another provider, but it must meet our minimum requirements.

Warning: Without adequate travel insurance, you will be responsible for all costs incurred due to medical emergencies, cancellations, or other unforeseen events.
