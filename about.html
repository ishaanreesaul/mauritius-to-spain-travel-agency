About SpainVoyage

Your trusted partner for unforgettable Spanish experiences from Mauritius

Our Story

Founded in 2009, SpainVoyage began with a simple mission: to make Spanish travel accessible and unforgettable for Mauritian travelers. What started as a small family business has grown into Mauritius' premier Spain travel specialist.

With over 15 years of experience, we've helped thousands of Mauritian families, couples, and solo travelers discover the magic of Spain. Our deep connections in Spain and understanding of Mauritian travel preferences make us uniquely positioned to create perfect Spanish adventures.
