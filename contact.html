Frequently Asked Questions

What are your office hours?

Our Port Louis office is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays and public holidays.

How quickly will I get a response?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our office directly.
