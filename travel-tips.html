Essential Tips by Category

Food & Dining

Spanish meal times are later than most countries. Lunch is typically 2-4 PM, dinner 9-11 PM. Try local specialties in each region. Tapas culture is best experienced by bar-hopping.

Transportation

Spain has excellent high-speed trains (AVE) connecting major cities. Purchase tickets in advance for better prices. In cities, use metro systems which are efficient and affordable.

Weather & Clothing

Pack layers as temperatures vary. Summer can be very hot, especially in Andalusia. Comfortable walking shoes are essential. Don't forget sunscreen and a hat for summer months.

Money Matters

Credit cards widely accepted. Carry some cash for small purchases. Tipping is not mandatory but appreciated. Service charge may be included in restaurants (servicio incluido).

Health & Safety

EU Health Insurance Card recommended. Pharmacies (farmacias) can help with minor ailments. Tap water is safe to drink. Spain is generally safe, but beware of pickpockets in tourist areas.

Festivals & Events

Check local festival calendars. Book accommodations early for major events like Semana Santa, La Tomatina, or Running of the Bulls. Some businesses close during local fiestas.

Regional Tips

Barcelona

Book Sagrada Familia tickets online in advance. Beware of pickpockets on Las Ramblas. Learn basic Catalan greetings.

Madrid

Visit museums on free entry days. Experience the Sunday flea market at El Rastro. Enjoy late-night tapas in La Latina.

Andalusia

Visit Alhambra with pre-booked tickets. Drink plenty of water in summer heat. Respect siesta time in smaller towns.

Coastal Areas

Apply sunscreen generously. Beach restaurants (chiringuitos) offer fresh seafood. Watersports equipment widely available.

For Mauritian Travelers

Flight Tips

Direct flights from Mauritius to Spain via major European hubs. Consider stopovers to break up long journey. Book 3-6 months in advance for best prices.

Climate Adaptation

Spanish winters colder than Mauritius. Summer heat can be intense. Pack appropriate clothing for seasonal variations.

Cultural Differences

Greetings usually with handshakes or two kisses. Dinner times much later than in Mauritius. Sundays many shops closed.
