Practical Information

Getting Around

City center is very walkable. Use tram or buses for longer distances. Taxis are affordable.

Dining Tips

Try salmorejo, pescaíto frito, and montaditos. Visit Triana market for tapas.

Festivals

Semana Santa (Holy Week) and Feria de Abril are major events. Book early.
