Practical Information

Essential travel tips and information for your trip to Spain

Money & Budgeting in Spain

Payment Methods

Credit Cards: Widely accepted (Visa, MasterCard)
Cash: Useful for small purchases
ATMs: Available everywhere (avoid airport ATMs)
Tipping: 5-10% in restaurants (optional)

Daily Budget

Budget: €50-70 per day
Mid-range: €70-150 per day
Luxury: €150+ per day
Food: €20-40 per day per person

Shopping

VAT Refund: Available for non-EU residents
Sales Tax: 21% (refundable for tourists)
Opening Hours: 10am-8pm (closed 2-5pm in some areas)
Bargaining: Not common except in markets

Health & Safety in Spain

Safety Tips

Pickpocketing: Be careful in tourist areas
Emergency: Dial 112 for any emergency
Police: 091 (National Police)
Tourist Police: Available in major cities

Healthcare

EU Citizens: EHIC card required
Non-EU: Travel insurance recommended
Pharmacies: Green cross sign, open 9am-10pm
Hospitals: High quality medical care available

Sun Protection

Suncream: SPF 30+ recommended
Hydration: Drink plenty of water
Peak Sun: Avoid 1pm-4pm in summer
Heat: Can reach 40°C in summer

Transport in Spain

Trains

High-speed (AVE): Madrid to Barcelona in 2.5h
Renfe: National railway company
Booking: Book in advance for discounts
Stations: Major cities have central stations

Buses

Intercity: ALSA is main operator
City buses: Efficient in all major cities
Tickets: Purchase at stations or online
Cost: Cheaper than trains

Taxis & Ride-sharing

Taxis: Metered, available 24/7
Uber: Available in major cities
Cabify: Popular local alternative
Fares: Displayed on meter

Communication in Spain

Internet & SIM Cards

Free WiFi: Available in cafes and hotels
SIM Cards: Purchase at airports or shops
Providers: Movistar, Vodafone, Orange
Roaming: EU roaming charges apply

Language

Official: Spanish (Castellano)
Regional: Catalan, Basque, Galician
English: Widely spoken in tourist areas
Useful Phrases: Hola, Gracias, Por favor

Time & Etiquette

Time Zone: CET (UTC+1)
Business Hours: 9am-2pm, 4pm-8pm
Meal Times: Late (lunch 2-4pm, dinner 9-11pm)
Greetings: Kiss on both cheeks common

Emergency Contacts

General Emergency

Dial 112 for any emergency

Police

091 (National Police)

Medical Emergency

061 (Ambulance)

Fire Department

080 (Fire Emergency)
