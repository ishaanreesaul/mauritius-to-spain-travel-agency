Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about traveling to Spain from Mauritius

Visa & Documentation

Do Mauritian citizens need a visa for Spain?

Yes, Mauritian citizens require a Schengen visa to visit Spain. The visa allows travel within the entire Schengen area for up to 90 days within a 180-day period.

What documents are needed for a Schengen visa application?

Required documents include:

  • Completed visa application form
  • Valid passport (minimum 3 months validity after return)
  • Passport-sized photos
  • Travel insurance with minimum €30,000 coverage
  • Flight itinerary
  • Hotel reservations
  • Proof of financial means
  • Employment letter or business registration

How long does the visa process take?

The standard processing time is 15 calendar days from application submission. During peak seasons (May-August), it may take up to 30 days. We recommend applying at least 3 months before your intended travel date.

Travel & Logistics

What is the best time to visit Spain from Mauritius?

The ideal times are:

  • Spring (April-June): Pleasant weather, fewer crowds
  • Autumn (September-October): Warm days, cool evenings
  • Summer (July-August): Hot, perfect for beach destinations but crowded
  • Winter (November-March): Cold in most regions, ski season in Pyrenees

How do I get around in Spain?

Spain has excellent transportation:

  • High-speed trains (AVE) between major cities
  • Domestic flights for longer distances
  • Efficient metro systems in cities
  • Buses for regional travel
  • Rental cars for flexibility (international license required)
Most of our packages include transportation between cities.

What should I pack for Spain?

Essentials include:

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Layered clothing (temperatures vary)
  • Sun protection (hat, sunglasses, sunscreen)
  • Adaptor for European plugs (Type C/F)
  • Copy of important documents
  • Basic medications
  • Modest clothing for visiting churches
